## 1️⃣ Brief description of your solution (≤1024 chars)

```
MittiAI turns a farmer's phone camera into an agronomist. Smallholders photograph a diseased leaf and Google Gemini's multimodal AI instantly returns the disease, confidence, severity, ICAR-dose treatment and a low-chemical regenerative plan — spoken aloud in their own language (Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, English). The same platform fuses real open data — Soil Health Card nutrients (data.gov.in), 12-week ISRO Bhuvan NDVI, 14-day IMD forecasts, Agmarknet mandi prices — into Gemini-generated regenerative crop calendars, and aggregates every diagnosis into a live district outbreak map with Gemini-drafted early-warning bulletins for agriculture officers. Voice-first, 2G-tolerant, no login. Built for India's 100M+ smallholders; the district data schema ports 1:1 across BRICS nations.
```
